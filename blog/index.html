Live like a local, not like a tourist

Polanco has the boutiques. La Condesa has the Instagram cafes. Roma has the rooftop bars. They're wonderful — and they're full of tourists. Anzures is where the people who actually live in Mexico City come home to. Tree-lined streets, Art Deco facades, corner bakeries where regulars don't need to order, and the legendary Taquería Selene five minutes from our door. The Museum of Anthropology is a 10-minute walk. Polanco's best restaurants, 5 minutes. Chapultepec Forest, 12. You're at the center of everything — without being in the middle of the crowd.

This guide is for people who want to experience Mexico City the way we do — not the version curated for visitors, but the real one. Where to eat, how to work remotely, what to do with kids, and why the best neighborhood in the city is the one nobody's heard of yet.
