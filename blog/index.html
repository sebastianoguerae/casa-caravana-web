Live like a local, not like a tourist

Polanco has the boutiques. La Condesa has the Instagram cafes. Roma has the rooftop bars. They're wonderful — and they're full of tourists. Anzures is where the people who actually live in Mexico City come home to — a quiet, residential neighborhood bordering Chapultepec Forest and just a 5-minute walk from Polanco. Tree-lined streets, Art Deco facades, corner bakeries, and the legendary Taquería Selene five minutes from our door.

This guide is for travelers who want to skip the tourist version of Mexico City and experience the real one. Where to eat, how to work remotely, what to do with kids, and why the best neighborhood in the city is the one that nobody's heard of — yet.
