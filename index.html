Our Story

The way our grandmothers made us feel

When we were children, there was nothing quite like arriving at our grandmothers' homes. The smell of something cooking, a bed made just for you, fresh towels laid out as if you were the most important guest in the world. Plants on every windowsill, flowers on the table — their homes were always alive. Cecilia, María Elvira, Isabel, and Silvia — four women who turned ordinary visits into extraordinary memories through the quiet art of making someone feel completely at home.

We built Casa Caravana to capture that feeling. Every apartment carries the name and personality of one of our grandmothers — not as a tribute on a wall, but as a living philosophy in every detail. The way the linens are chosen, the coffee waiting in the kitchen, the neighborhood guide on your nightstand. These are gestures we learned from watching them care for the people they loved.

Mariana, our host and Superhost with 195+ reviews and a 4.91 rating, carries this spirit forward every single day. When you stay with us, you are not arriving at just another place in Mexico City. You are being welcomed the way our grandmothers welcomed us — with warmth, attention, and the kind of care that makes you feel like family.

Anzures — The locals' best-kept secret

Casa Caravana sits in Anzures, a residential neighborhood that most tourists never discover — and that's exactly why we love it. While Polanco dazzles with its boutiques and Michelin-starred restaurants just five minutes away, Anzures offers something rarer: the authentic rhythm of a Mexico City neighborhood where real families live.

Here you'll find the corner bakery where neighbors line up for fresh conchas every morning, the family-run taquería that hasn't changed in decades, the quiet tree-lined streets where you can actually hear birds. The local market, the pharmacy, the coffee shop where the barista remembers your order — this is the Mexico City that postcards don't show, but the one you'll remember most.

And yet, you're never more than a few minutes from everything: Polanco, Chapultepec, Reforma, the best museums in Latin America. Anzures gives you both worlds — the soul of the city and the ease of its center.

The Neighborhood

Your guide to Polanco & beyond

Polanco

The city's most cosmopolitan neighborhood. Art galleries, designer boutiques, Michelin-starred restaurants, and the famous Avenida Presidente Masaryk await you just five minutes on foot from Casa Caravana.

Museum of Anthropology

One of the most important museums in the world, a ten-minute walk away. It houses the Sun Stone and thousands of pieces that tell the story of Mesoamerican civilizations.

Chapultepec Forest

The city's green lung. Over 680 hectares for running, cycling, or simply strolling among centuries-old ahuehuete trees. Chapultepec Castle offers unforgettable panoramic views.

Avenida Reforma

Mexico's most iconic avenue. It connects the city's main points of interest and every Sunday transforms into an open bike lane for thousands of cyclists.

Local gastronomy

From tacos de canasta on the corner to some of the best restaurants in Latin America. Pujol, Quintonil, and dozens of options for every taste, all minutes from your door.

Nightlife & culture

Mezcal bars in Polanco, live jazz in Condesa, theater on Reforma. Mexico City never sleeps — and you're at the center of it all.
